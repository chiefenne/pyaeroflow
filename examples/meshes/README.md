cases (channel, cavity, cylinder, plates) are generated by
`examples/cases/make_meshes.py` and `examples/cases/make_plate_lm.py` and are
not committed.

In the PyAero names `C10` is the tunnel height of 10 chords above and below
the airfoil and `W25` the wake length of 25 chords behind the trailing edge.
The chord is 1. The PyAero meshes are not scaled for a Reynolds number; check
y⁺ with `pyaeroflow check-mesh MESH --re R`. The RAE 2822 mesh has capitalised
boundary names: use them in the `[boundary.<name>]` sections of a case file.

`make_nasa_hump.py` converts a TMR no-plenum hump grid (one block) to `.su2`
and cuts the suction slot exit out of the wall; the 205 x 55 and 817 x 217
grids give the same slot (1 and 4 faces, the same L = 0.00456 c).

`split_marker.py` moves the faces of a marker in an x range (upper, lower or
both sides) to a new marker, e.g. a slot for a `transpiration-wall`:
`python split_marker.py IN.su2 OUT.su2 airfoil slot XMIN XMAX upper`. The
slot has whole wall faces, so its length depends on the surface spacing; the
script prints it.

`make_tmr_naca0012.py` converts the TMR NACA 0012 PLOT3D grid to `.su2` and
rebuilds the finer TMR levels (449 × 129, 897 × 257), whose files are not in
the public TMR repository: `python make_tmr_naca0012.py
naca0012_tmr_225x65.p2dfmt n0012_449.su2 1` (`2` for 897 × 257). The refined
grids match TMR's first-cell heights (1.7·10⁻⁶ and 8.1·10⁻⁷ c) but are not
identical to the TMR grids.

`make_sd7003_ogrid.py` needs the wall CSV of a run on the SD7003 C-mesh
(`python make_sd7003_ogrid.py WALL.csv OUT.su2 [n_surface] [n_radial]`); the
committed O-grid is the result.

## Local refinement demo

`refine_demo.py` refines an airfoil mesh in fixed regions (wall cells up to
x = `--le` split along the wall, and the trailing-edge region within `--te`)
and writes a `.vtu` for ParaView with the cell fields `split_u`, `split_v`,
`n_nodes` (5 or more = hanging nodes), `aspect_ratio`, `cell_area` and
`origin`. Example:

    python examples/meshes/refine_demo.py examples/meshes/sd7003_C10_W25_320k_cells.su2 sd7003_refined.vtu --walls airfoil
